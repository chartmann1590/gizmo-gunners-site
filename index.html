FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really offline?

Yes. Every match is you against computer-controlled robots, played entirely on your phone. No internet connection is needed to play, there is no lag, no cheaters and no account to make. Ads and purchases need a connection when they appear, but the game itself does not.

Is it free?

Gizmo Gunners is free to download. It shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases: Remove Ads ($2.99), coin and gem packs ($0.99 to $9.99) and cosmetic hero skins.

Is it pay-to-win?

No. Coins and gems are earned by playing: daily rewards, daily and weekly challenges, hidden secrets and mission rewards. Every hero and squad mate can be unlocked without paying. There are no loot boxes and no subscriptions. The Gizmo Pass has a free lane for everyone; the premium lane adds cosmetics and extra currency. Packs only speed up what you would earn by playing anyway.

Where do ads show up?

One banner on a few menu screens only, never during gameplay. A full-screen ad can appear after a mission ends (never in your first two matches, at most every other match and a few minutes apart), never in the middle of a fight. Reward videos are optional: watch one to revive once, double your coins or rehire a squad mate. Buying Remove Ads turns off the banners and the full-screen ads.

What phones does it run on?

Android phones, with Low, Medium and High graphics settings and a 30 or 60 fps option so it can run smoothly on older phones and look great on newer ones.

Is it for kids?

It is rated for teens because of cartoon violence. Robots burst into sparks and bolts; there is no blood. It is not designed for children.

Which languages?

The game is available in 21 languages.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud save. Uninstalling the app or using Reset progress in Settings deletes it. Purchases are tied to your Google Play account and can be restored.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Open an issue on our GitHub page. There are templates for bug reports, feature ideas and privacy questions.
