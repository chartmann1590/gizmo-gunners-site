Delete your data

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Last updated: October 15, 2026 · App: Gizmo Gunners: Toon Bot Shooter (package com.charles.gizmogunners) · Developer: Hartmann Studios

Gizmo Gunners has no account. Everything the game stores lives only on your phone, so you delete it yourself, right on the phone. There is nothing on our side to delete.

Delete your game data

Option 1: in the game. Open Settings → Reset progress and confirm. This deletes your progress, coins, gems, upgrades and settings from your phone immediately.
Option 2: uninstall. Uninstalling Gizmo Gunners deletes everything the game stored. You can also go to Android Settings → Apps → Gizmo Gunners → Storage → Clear storage.

What is deleted

  • Game progress: missions, stars, best Survival wave, unlocked heroes, weapon upgrades, daily mission and login-calendar state.
  • Coins and gems you have not spent, and your settings.

What is kept

  • Purchases. Remove Ads and hero skins are tied to your Google Play account, not to the game's storage. After reinstalling, tap Restore purchases in Settings to get them back. To manage your Google Play purchase history, use your Google account.
  • Ad data held by Google. Google AdMob may hold data linked to your advertising ID under Google's policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

Need help?

If anything is unclear, open an issue on GitHub using the "Privacy or data question" template. Please don't post personal information in the issue.
